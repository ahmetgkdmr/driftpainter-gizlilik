Privacy Policy

Drift Painter is made by Ahmet Gokdemir. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

  • Your progress (levels, credits, cars, paintings and settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Game Center (optional): if you are signed in, your arena scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards with your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your Apple ID.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • Photos: the game asks for permission only to add your paintings to your photo library when you tap Save. It cannot read your photos.
  • Notifications (optional): the daily reminder is scheduled on your device. No data leaves your device.

Drift Painter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

Support

Questions, bugs or purchase problems? Email driftpainter@ahmetgokdemir.com. If a purchase did not arrive, open the shop (BUY CREDITS) and tap RESTORE.
